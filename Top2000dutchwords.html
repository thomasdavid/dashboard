About This Tool

This flashcard system is designed to help you master the 4,621 most frequently used Dutch words. The frequency list is based on the massive database of movie and television subtitles from www.opensubtitles.org.

How it works:

  • Front of Card: Displays the Dutch word, its grammatical type, and a sentence for context.
  • First Reveal: Click the card or press Spacebar to see the Dutch definition.
  • Second Reveal: Click again to show the English translation.

Use the difficulty buttons below to filter the deck based on frequency and word length. Every time you pick a level, the words are randomly shuffled so you never review them in the same order twice!
